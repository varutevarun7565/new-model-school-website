PARENT CORNER

Questions parents often ask.

Have another question? Our team will be happy to help.

Ask us directly →
What makes your learning books different?+

New Model School uses its own-designed learning materials created with a focus on foundational concepts and age-appropriate practice. Contact us to learn more about the books used for each level.

What if my child cannot eat independently or use the washroom?+

Every child develops at a different pace. Please discuss your child's needs with the school before admission so the team can explain the support and routines available. We encourage parents to share relevant information openly.

How do you support children with short attention spans?+

Young children often learn best through short, varied activities. Our approach aims to include hands-on practice, creative tasks, repetition with purpose, and opportunities to participate. Individual needs can be discussed with the school.

When will the new branch open, and which classes will it offer?+

The new branch is planned for the 2027–2028 academic year, with Playgroup to Standard 5 proposed. The location, final class offerings, and admissions schedule are not yet confirmed.

How can I arrange a school visit?

Use the school visit request form below or contact us by phone. The school will get in touch to confirm an available time.
